Archonex Converter

Convert any file, any size. No limits, and nothing leaves your device.

Most free converters cap you at 100 MB and upload your files to somebody's server. This one converts on your own machine, at the full technical ceiling of the platform — 1 TB on desktop, 2 GB on Android — and those are real hardware limits, not a discounted offering.

What it converts

  • Video and audio — MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, GIF, MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG and more, with quality presets and per-file overrides.
  • Images, in batches — JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, TIFF. Thirty photos on mobile, a hundred on desktop, in one run.
  • PDF, both directions — photos or text into a PDF, or a PDF back out as images.

Linux carries no media engine, so only the PDF converter runs there — the app says so on screen rather than failing halfway. Every ceiling above is the platform's real maximum; the reasoning behind each number is in the source.

Honest limitations

  • Text to PDF covers Latin, Greek and Cyrillic. Chinese, Japanese and Korean are refused with a clear message rather than written out as blank boxes.
  • HEIC and AVIF can be read, not written. No encoder for them ships in the bundled engine.
  • Very large files on mobile can run out of memory. Saving a result can hold two or three times its size at once, and 2 GB is the platform ceiling, not a safety margin.
