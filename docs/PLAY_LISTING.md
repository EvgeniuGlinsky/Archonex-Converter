### Full description (4000 max)

**English**
```
Archonex Converter converts files on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is queued behind
somebody else's server, and there is no artificial cap on how large a file can be.

Most free converters stop at 100 MB and send your files away to do the work. This one runs the
conversion locally, at the real technical ceiling of your device — up to 2 GB per file on Android.

WHAT IT CONVERTS

• Video and audio — MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, GIF, MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG and more. Quality presets for
  the common cases, and per-file control over resolution, frame rate, bitrate and audio when you want
  it.
• Images, in batches — JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP and TIFF, up to thirty photos in a single run. A failure on
  one photo stays on that photo instead of ending the batch.
• PDF, in both directions — turn photos or text into a PDF, or take a PDF apart into images.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

• Fully offline. Your files never leave the device, so nothing can be retained, leaked or read by
  anyone else.
• No account. Nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
• No adverts, no tracking, no analytics of any kind.
• Open source, under the Apache 2.0 licence.

FREE AND PAID

The free tier includes ten source files every calendar month — every converter, every format and
every file size, with nothing held back. The count refills on the 1st.

Only conversions that actually succeed are counted. A run you cancel, a run that fails and an
unreadable photo in a batch of thirty all cost nothing.

A subscription lifts the count entirely: $0.99 per month, or $7.99 per year. Subscriptions are billed
through Google Play and can be cancelled there at any time.

HONEST LIMITATIONS

We would rather tell you these up front than have you discover them:

• Text to PDF covers Latin, Greek and Cyrillic. Chinese, Japanese and Korean text is refused with a
  clear message rather than drawn as blank boxes.
• HEIC and AVIF can be read as sources but not written as results — no encoder for them ships in the
  bundled engine.
• Very large files can run out of memory on some devices. Saving a result can briefly hold two to
  three times its size, and 2 GB is the platform's hard ceiling rather than a comfortable margin.

Archonex Converter is also available for Windows and Linux. See the project page for details.
```

$0.99; a handful of markets allow less.

## Data safety

The app collects and shares nothing, which makes this section short. Answers:

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | n/a — no data is collected |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted? | n/a — no data is collected |

Notes to keep to hand if a reviewer asks:

- Files selected by the user are read and written locally, never transmitted. There is no server.
- Local preferences hold only the interface language, the monthly conversion count and whether the
  device is subscribed. None of it identifies the user, and it is removed on uninstall.
- The only network use is Google Play billing, which Google handles as seller of record.
